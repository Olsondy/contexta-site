The library keeps several handwritten field journals in a climate-controlled room because researchers still consider them highly prized records of the region’s early plant life.

The word “prized” in the passage is closest in meaning to:

  • Avalued
  • Buniversal
  • Cuncommon
  • Dpreserved

最强迷惑项对比

uncommon 只说“不常见”；prized 强调“被珍惜、被认为有价值”，对应 valued。
